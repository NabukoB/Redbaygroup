Building inspection

Know what is wrong before you pay or repair.

Thinking of buying a house, taking over from a contractor, or worried about cracks and leaks? We inspect the building and give you a written report: what is wrong, how serious it is, what to fix first and what it may cost.

What you get

A clear written report

  • A site visit and a walk through the whole building, inside and outside, roof included where it is safe to reach
  • Photos of every defect we find, with its location marked
  • Each defect rated: urgent, fix soon or cosmetic, so you know where to start
  • Common problems checked: cracks, damp and leaks, roof and gutters, plaster and finishes, floors, doors and windows, plumbing and electrical points
  • Rough repair costs and what the repair involves, so you can negotiate a price or hold back a payment
  • A clear note when something looks structural and needs a registered structural engineer

When people call us

  • Before buying a house or a building
  • When a contractor says the job is finished and you want a second opinion before the final payment
  • Cracks in walls, floors or ceilings
  • Leaks, damp patches or peeling paint
  • A build that does not look right halfway through

Our report is a contractor's inspection. It is not a structural certificate. If we think the structure may be at risk, we say so clearly and tell you to bring in a registered structural engineer.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you tell me if a building is safe?

+

We can tell you what we can see: cracks, damp, poor workmanship and signs of trouble. We cannot certify structural safety. A registered structural engineer does that, and we tell you when you need one.

How long does it take and what does it cost?

+

For an ordinary house the visit is usually a few hours and the report follows after. Tell us the location and size of the building and we will quote the fee and the timing before we come.

Will you also fix what you find?

+

If you want us to, we will quote the repairs. You do not have to hire us. The report is yours to use with any builder.

Can you inspect a building that is not finished?

+

Yes. This is a good time to find a problem. For a build that is still going on, our site supervision service checks it at each stage.
