The questions

Ask these before you sign

  1. 1

    Are you registered with the National Construction Authority, and in which category?

    Ask to see the certificate and check that it is current and covers the size of your project.

  2. 2

    Can I see finished work like mine and speak to two past clients?

    A good contractor is happy to show you. Visit a finished or running site if you can.

  3. 3

    Who will supervise my site, and how often will they be there?

    Ask for a name. Work goes wrong when nobody in charge is watching it.

  4. 4

    Will you quote against a bill of quantities, with labour and materials shown separately?

    A single lump sum with no breakdown makes it impossible to compare quotations or to price changes.

  5. 5

    What is not included in your price?

    Ask about finishes, fittings, the perimeter wall, connections and approvals, so there are no surprises later.

  6. 6

    How will you price changes and extras?

    Agree in writing, before the work is done, what each change costs.

  7. 7

    What is the payment schedule, and is it tied to stages I can inspect?

    Pay for stages you have seen, such as foundation, slab, walls and roof, not a large amount upfront.

  8. 8

    What is the programme, and what happens if the work runs late?

    Ask for a start date and dates for each stage, and agree what the contractor does if they are missed.

  9. 9

    Which materials will you use, and can I see the specification?

    Ask for the thickness, grade and brand in writing, and check the manufacturer's stamp and the standards mark.

  10. 10

    What guarantee do you give after handover, and is it in writing?

    Ask what is covered, for how long, and who to call when something fails.

Walk away if

Red flags

  • Asks for a large payment before any work is done
  • Gives one lump-sum price with no breakdown
  • Cannot show a registration certificate or finished work
  • Pressure to start before drawings and approvals are ready
  • A price far below every other quotation
