Our story

From one contractor to a group.

Redbay started building in 2019 and was incorporated as Redbay Construction Ltd in 2022. Since then we have built schools and family homes in Isinya, Lukenya, Nanyuki, Nyahururu, Kakamega and Diani, from our base in Ruaraka, Nairobi.

As our work grew, so did what clients asked of us: the finishes inside a building, and the power that runs it. Redbay Group brings that work together through four specialised companies: Redbay Construction, Redbay Schools, Redbay Interiors and Redbay Energy.

We keep a small permanent team that runs every site, and bring in trusted tradespeople for each project. It keeps us close to the work and easy to reach.

Our process

How a project runs

You know the price, the scope and the timeline before any work starts.

  1. 1

    Site visit and brief

    We walk the site, read your plans and agree what is being built.

  2. 2

    Bill of quantities

    Every item measured and priced, so you can see where the money goes.

  3. 3

    Fixed quotation

    One clear total, with the scope, timeline and payment stages written down.

  4. 4

    Build and supervise

    Our team and trusted tradespeople build, with supervision on site.

  5. 5

    Inspection and handover

    We walk through the finished work with you and fix what needs fixing.
