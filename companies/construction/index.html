What we do

Redbay Construction services

Residential construction and development, delivering quality homes built for durability, functionality and modern living.

  • New homes

    Maisonettes, bungalows and villas, from setting out to handover.

  • Perimeter walls and gates

    Stone and block boundary walls, built straight and ready for gates and fencing.

  • Roofing and flat roofs

    New roofs, re-roofing and waterproofed flat roofs that drain properly.

  • Cabro paving and external works

    Driveways, parking and walkways laid on a compacted, graded base.

  • Earthworks and backfilling

    Site clearing, levelling and backfilling to the levels on your drawings.

  • Renovations and extensions

    Adding rooms or floors, and bringing older homes up to date.

Our process

How a project runs

You know the price, the scope and the timeline before any work starts.

  1. 1

    Site visit and brief

    We walk the site, read your plans and agree what is being built.

  2. 2

    Bill of quantities

    Every item measured and priced, so you can see where the money goes.

  3. 3

    Fixed quotation

    One clear total, with the scope, timeline and payment stages written down.

  4. 4

    Build and supervise

    Our team and trusted tradespeople build, with supervision on site.

  5. 5

    Inspection and handover

    We walk through the finished work with you and fix what needs fixing.
