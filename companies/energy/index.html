Our process

How a project runs

You know the price, the scope and the timeline before any work starts.

  1. 1

    Site visit and brief

    We walk the site, read your plans and agree what is being built.

  2. 2

    Bill of quantities

    Every item measured and priced, so you can see where the money goes.

  3. 3

    Fixed quotation

    One clear total, with the scope, timeline and payment stages written down.

  4. 4

    Build and supervise

    Our team and trusted tradespeople build, with supervision on site.

  5. 5

    Inspection and handover

    We walk through the finished work with you and fix what needs fixing.
