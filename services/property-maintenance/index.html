Specialised service

Property maintenance and repairs

Buildings wear out fastest when small faults are left. We inspect your property, list what needs attention in order of importance, and repair it, so a small leak or crack does not become an expensive one.

Signs you need it

  • Damp stains, leaks or peeling paint on walls and ceilings
  • Cracked plaster, broken tiles or lifting floor finishes
  • Blocked gutters or drains, and water standing around the building
  • Rusted grills, loose doors or damaged ceilings
  • A building that has not been inspected for years

Where we work

Areas we cover

  • Homes and rental properties

    Repairs and repainting for family homes, and upkeep for landlords and agents, including work between tenants.

  • Schools and institutions

    Repairs to classrooms, roofs, floors and toilets, scheduled around the school calendar so lessons are not disturbed.

  • Offices and commercial buildings

    Repairs to ceilings, partitions, floors and finishes, done in the evenings and at weekends where needed.

  • Before letting or selling

    Fixing leaks, repainting and repairing finishes so a property is ready for new tenants or buyers.

Methods

How we do it

Planned inspections

A walk-through of the whole property, with photos and a list of defects ranked by importance.

Routine repairs

Plaster, paint, tiles, ceilings, doors and grills repaired and finished to match what is there.

Leaks and drainage

Roof repairs, waterproofing, gutters and drains, fixed at the source rather than patched over.

Regular maintenance visits

A visit at an agreed interval to inspect and fix small items, so problems are caught early.

How we work

From first visit to handover

  1. 1We inspect the property and list what needs attention, ranked by importance
  2. 2You get an itemised quote, with labour and materials shown separately
  3. 3We agree the priorities, the order of work and the dates, including times that suit the people using the building
  4. 4We carry out the repairs with as little disruption as possible
  5. 5We send a short report with before and after photos, and agree when to inspect again
