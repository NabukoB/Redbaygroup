Services

Specialised services.

Alongside complete builds, we take on specialised work for new and existing buildings.

  • Architectural and structural drawingsEvery approved building starts with drawings. We prepare the floor plans, elevations and sections, and the structural drawings for the foundations, columns, beams, slabs and roof, in the form that approval submissions need.Learn more
  • Cost estimation and planning (BoQs)An itemised bill of quantities measured from your drawings, so you know the real cost before you build.Learn more
  • WaterproofingLeaks and damp damage plaster, paint, ceilings and, in time, the structure itself. We find where the water is getting in, fix the cause, and waterproof it properly, in new buildings and existing ones.Learn more
  • RoofingA roof keeps the whole building dry. We build new roofs to your drawings, replace worn-out roofs, and repair leaks at the source, with the timber and fixings sized to hold.Learn more
  • Project managementA building project goes wrong when nobody is watching the programme, the budget and the quality at the same time. We plan the work, keep it on programme and on budget, supervise on site, and report to you in plain language.Learn more
  • Site audits and inspectionBefore you pay for the next stage, or buy or take over a building, it helps to know what is really there. We inspect the work on site, compare it with the drawings and the bill of quantities, and give you a written report on what is right and what needs fixing.Learn more
  • Site setupA building site needs a secure place for materials, a toilet for the workers, security and a boundary before the first foundation is dug. We set all four up together, so the site is organised and protected from day one.Learn more
  • Property maintenanceBuildings wear out fastest when small faults are left. We inspect your property, list what needs attention in order of importance, and repair it, so a small leak or crack does not become an expensive one.Learn more
