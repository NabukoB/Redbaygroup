Architectural and structural drawings

Every approved building starts with drawings. We prepare the floor plans, elevations and sections, and the structural drawings for the foundations, columns, beams, slabs and roof, in the form that approval submissions need.

Signs you need it

  • You have a plot but no drawings yet
  • You need drawings to submit for building approval
  • You have a plan from another source that needs structural design
  • You want to check a plan before you build from it

Where we work

Areas we cover

  • New house plans

    Architectural drawings for a new home, school or building, designed around your plot, budget and the way you will use the space.

  • Structural drawings

    The foundations, columns, beams, slabs and roof frame, designed to carry the loads on the building.

  • Approval submissions

    Drawings prepared for building approval, with the sets and details that the approving authority asks for.

  • Reviewing your own plan

    Checking a plan you already have for structure, layout and approvals before you build from it.

Methods

The right system for each area

Architectural drawings

Floor plans, elevations, sections and the site layout, showing how the building sits on the plot.

Structural drawings

Foundation, column, beam, slab and roof details, with the sizes and reinforcement the structure needs.

Approval-ready sets

A complete set of drawings in the form the approving authority asks for, so the submission is not sent back for missing details.

How we work

From inspection to tested finish

  1. 1We meet you, visit the plot and agree the brief, the size and the budget
  2. 2You get a fixed quote for each set of drawings, with the work listed separately
  3. 3We prepare the architectural drawings, then the structural design and drawings
  4. 4Where the approving authority requires it, an architect signs the drawings
  5. 5We help you through the approval submission and make any changes the authority asks for
FAQ

Common questions

Do the drawings need an architect's signature?

+

Where the approving authority requires it, yes. We work with a registered architect for that, and we say so in your quote, so you know who signs and what it costs.

How much do drawings cost?

+

Each set is quoted separately, based on the size and complexity of the building. Drawings are priced as a fixed fee in your quote.

Can you draw a house from my own sketch or plan?

+

Yes. We can turn a sketch or plan into full drawings, and check it for structure and approvals before you build.
