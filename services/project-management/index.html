Specialised service

Project management for building projects

A building project goes wrong when nobody is watching the programme, the budget and the quality at the same time. We plan the work, keep it on programme and on budget, supervise on site, and report to you in plain language.

Signs you need it

  • You are building but cannot be on site often
  • Costs keep rising and nobody can say why
  • The work is behind programme and no one has a new date
  • Several tradespeople are working and nobody is coordinating them
  • You want to build in phases and need the order of work planned

Where we work

Areas we cover

  • Family homes

    Planning and managing a home build from setting out to handover, in one go or in phases as your funds allow.

  • Schools and institutions

    Phased building programmes planned around a school that keeps running, with work scheduled to cause the least disruption.

  • Renovations and fit-outs

Methods

How we do it

Programme and phasing

A stage-by-stage plan showing the order of work, who does what, and when each stage should finish.

Budget control

Spending checked against the bill of quantities, with any change priced and agreed in writing before the work is done.

Site supervision

Regular checks on site that the work follows the drawings, the specification and good practice.

Progress reports

Written updates with photos, covering progress against the programme and money spent against the budget.

Inspection and handover

A walk-through of the finished work, a list of items to fix, and a check that they are fixed.

How we work

From first visit to handover

  1. 1We review your drawings, bill of quantities and budget, and agree the scope with you
  2. 2We set the programme and the order of work, and agree how and when we report
  3. 3We supervise the work on site and keep the budget under control
  4. 4We send you regular written updates with photos
  5. 5We inspect the finished work, list what needs fixing, and hand over once it is done
FAQ

Common questions

Can you manage a project that someone else is building?

+

Yes, where you want an independent person watching the programme, the budget and the quality. We agree the scope with you in writing first, so everyone knows what we are responsible for.

What will I receive?

+

Regular written updates with photos, showing progress against the programme and spending against the budget, plus a list of anything that needs your decision.

How is project management priced?

+

We agree the fee and the scope in writing before we start. It is quoted separately from the cost of building, so you can see what each part costs.
