Specialised service

Site audits and building inspections

Before you pay for the next stage, or buy or take over a building, it helps to know what is really there. We inspect the work on site, compare it with the drawings and the bill of quantities, and give you a written report on what is right and what needs fixing.

Signs you need it

  • You are about to pay for a stage and cannot judge the work yourself
  • Cracks, leaks or uneven floors in a new building
  • Costs or quantities that do not match the bill of quantities
  • You are buying or renovating an older building
  • A project has stalled and you need to know what has been done

Where we work

Areas we cover

  • Work in progress

    Stage checks before you pay, such as foundations, slab, walls and roof, to confirm the work matches what was agreed.

  • Finished work

    A handover inspection of a new or renovated building, with a list of items that need fixing.

  • Existing buildings

    A condition check before you buy, rent or renovate an older building, so you know what you are taking on.

  • Quotes and contractors' claims

    Checking measured work against a quotation or bill of quantities, so you pay for what has been done.

Methods

How we do it

Measured inspection

Checking sizes, levels and finishes on site against the drawings and the specification.

Quantity check

Measuring completed work and comparing it with the bill of quantities and what has been claimed.

Photo report

A written report with photos and findings ranked by importance, so you know what to fix first.

Snag list and re-check

A list of defects to be fixed, and a second visit to confirm they have been fixed properly.

How we work

From first visit to handover

  1. 1We agree the scope with you and collect the drawings and the bill of quantities
  2. 2We visit the site and inspect and measure the work
  3. 3You receive a written report with photos and a ranked list of findings
  4. 4We walk you through the report and advise on what to do next
  5. 5After repairs, we visit again to confirm the work is done properly
FAQ

Common questions

Do you give a structural certificate?

+

No. We report what we see on site. Where there is a structural concern, we say so and recommend that a registered engineer looks at it.

Can you inspect work done by another contractor?

+

Yes. We check the work against the drawings and the bill of quantities and report what we find, without taking sides.

What do I get at the end?

+

A written report with photos, a ranked list of issues, and a plain explanation of what each one means and what to do about it.
