Specialised service

Site setup: site house, toilet, security and perimeter

A building site needs a secure place for materials, a toilet for the workers, security and a boundary before the first foundation is dug. We set all four up together, so the site is organised and protected from day one.

Signs you need it

  • You are about to start building and have no safe place for materials
  • Cement, tools or fittings have gone missing from the site
  • Workers have no toilet on site
  • The plot is open and anyone can walk in
  • You live far from the site and want to see what happens on it

Where we work

Areas we cover

  • Site house

    A lockable room on site for cement, tools and fittings, and for the foreman and the drawings, built on a raised floor to keep it dry.

  • Site toilet

    A toilet for the workers, placed away from the building area and the neighbours, so the site stays clean and the crew has what it needs.

  • Security and CCTV

    Cameras covering the gate, the materials store and the work area, with a recorder, so you can see who comes and goes.

  • Perimeter walling

    The plot closed off from the start: a barricade for the building period, or the permanent perimeter wall built first.

Methods

How we do it

Site house on a raised floor

A simple, lockable structure with a raised floor, so cement and fittings stay dry and secure.

Site toilet

A basic toilet built or placed on site, kept away from the work area and the plot boundary.

CCTV cameras and recorder

Cameras fixed at the gate and the store, wired to a recorder, with the cabling protected from weather and damage.

Barricade or permanent wall

Iron sheets on posts for a quick barricade that comes down after the build, or a permanent wall built first and kept as the final boundary.

How we work

From first visit to handover

  1. 1We visit the plot and agree where the gate, site house, toilet and cameras will go
  2. 2You get an itemised quote, with labour and materials shown separately for each part
  3. 3We close off the plot first, with a gate that locks
  4. 4We build the site house and toilet, then install and test the cameras
  5. 5We hand over the keys and show you how the system works, then plan what stays and what comes down after the build
FAQ

Common questions

Do I really need a site house?

+

It is not required on every project, but materials left in the open get wet, damaged or stolen. A lockable site house protects them and gives the foreman a dry place for the drawings.

Can I watch the cameras from my phone?

+

Where the site has a network connection, remote viewing can be set up. Tell us when you ask for the quote.

Is the perimeter a temporary barricade or a permanent wall?

+

Either. A barricade is quicker and cheaper and comes down after building. A permanent wall can be built first so it becomes your final boundary. Our perimeter wall calculator gives a quick estimate for a permanent wall.

Is site setup included in the bill of quantities?
