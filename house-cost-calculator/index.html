Where the numbers come from

  • Institute of Quantity Surveyors of Kenya (IQSK) building cost figures, as reported in October 2025
  • Nairobi and Central: KSh 48,750 per m² for a standard bungalow
  • Nairobi and Central: KSh 84,000 per m² for a luxury maisonette

How building costs are worked out

How much does it cost to build a house in Kenya per square metre?

+

Published figures for Nairobi and Central put a standard bungalow at about KSh 48,750 per m² and a luxury maisonette at about KSh 84,000 per m². The real figure for your house depends on your drawings and finishes.

Is this a Redbay price?

+

No. It is a published industry guide, so you can plan a budget. We give our own price after pricing your drawings in a bill of quantities.

How do I get an exact cost?

+

Send us your drawings. We prepare a bill of quantities that lists every item with quantities and one fixed price.
