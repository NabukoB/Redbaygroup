BoQ service

Know the real cost before you build.

Send us your drawings and we will prepare an itemised bill of quantities: every item measured and priced, so you can budget, build in phases and compare quotes with confidence.

What you get

An itemised BoQ for your building

  • Every item measured from your drawings: substructure, walling, roofing, finishes, plumbing, electrical and external works
  • Current rates for materials and labour, item by item
  • Totals for each stage, so you can plan building in phases
  • A clear list of what is included and what is not
  • A summary you can use to compare other builders' quotes line by line

What to send us

  • Architectural drawings: plans, elevations and sections
  • Structural drawings, if you have them
  • The site location
  • The finishes you have in mind: basic, standard or high-end

We confirm the fee before we start. The BoQ is yours to keep and use, whether or not you build with us.

FAQ

Common questions

Why do I need a bill of quantities?

+

A BoQ tells you what your building will really cost before you start, shows you where the money goes, and lets you compare builders fairly. Without one, cheap quotes often leave items out that come back later as extra costs.

How much does a BoQ cost?

+

It depends on the size of the building and how complete the drawings are. Send us the drawings and we will quote the fee before we start.

Do I have to hire you to build?

+

No. The BoQ is yours to use however you choose, including to get quotes from other builders. If you do build with us, it becomes the basis of our fixed quotation.

Can you prepare a BoQ without drawings?

+

We need drawings to measure accurately. If you do not have them yet, we can give you a rough budget from a description of the house and advise on getting drawings done.
