A guide, not a quote. Hips, valleys, dormers and sheets that run beyond the roof edge change the count. We confirm the exact order after seeing the roof or the drawings.

The method

How the count is worked out

  • Roof width divided by the effective cover of one sheet, rounded up
  • Effective cover, not full width, because neighbouring sheets overlap
  • Each sheet runs ridge to eaves, with the overhang added
  • A few spare sheets for cutting and damage
  • Ridge caps, flashings and fixings counted separately
Types of iron sheets explained

Why does the count use effective cover?

+

A corrugated sheet is 865 mm wide but covers 762 mm once it overlaps its neighbour. Counting by full width gives too few sheets.

How many sheets for a 10 m by 4 m roof?

+

With two slopes of 10 m by 4 m in corrugated sheet, you need 14 sheets across each slope, so 28 sheets, each 4 m long, plus a few spare.

Does this include price?

+

No. Sheet prices change often with gauge, profile and finish. Send us the count and we quote the materials and labour together.
