How it works

What the numbers assume

  • Blocks are 400 × 200 mm on the face, which is 12.5 blocks per square metre of wall before joints. This is slightly generous.
  • Spare: 5% added to blocks, cement, sand and ballast for breakage, cutting and waste.
  • Concrete: the dry materials needed are 1.54 times the wet volume. Cement weighs 1,440 kg per cubic metre and comes in 50 kg bags.
  • Mortar, plaster and screed are not included. Steel is not included, because it comes from the structural drawings.
Read the guide: how many blocks and bags of cement

How many blocks do I need per square metre of wall?

+

About 12.5 for a 400 × 200 mm block, before allowing for spares. Multiply your wall area, after taking out doors and windows, by 12.5 and add about 5%.

How many bags of cement are in a cubic metre of concrete?

+

For a 1 : 2 : 4 mix, about 6 to 7 bags of 50 kg, depending on the allowance for waste. A richer mix such as 1 : 1.5 : 3 needs more cement and a leaner one needs less.

Is this the same as a bill of quantities?

+

No. A bill of quantities lists every item from your drawings with labour and materials shown separately. This calculator gives quick quantities for planning and for checking a quotation.
