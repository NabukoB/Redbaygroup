  • Setting out the boundary line

  • Reinforced concrete columns at regular spacing
  • Walling between the columns, 2.4 m high
  • A ring beam on top to tie the wall together
What changes the price

How much does a perimeter wall cost per metre in Kenya?

+

Redbay builds standard perimeter walls from KSh 14,000 per running metre, for a wall 2.4 metres high on a foundation 0.8 metres deep, before VAT. We confirm the exact rate after seeing the site.

How long is the boundary of a 50 by 100 plot?

+

A 50 × 100 ft plot (an eighth of an acre) has a boundary of about 91 metres, so a full wall would start from about KSh 1.27 million before the gate.

Is the gate included?

+

No. Gates vary a lot in size and style, so we price the gate separately once you have chosen one.
