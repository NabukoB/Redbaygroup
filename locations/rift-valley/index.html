What to plan for in Nakuru and Kericho

  • Rain most of the year in Kericho

    Kericho is wet and cool for much of the year. Roofs need the right pitch, properly lapped sheets or tiles, sound flashings at gables and eaves, and gutters sized for heavy rain.

  • Roof structure first

    A hipped and gabled roof is only as good as its frame. Steel trusses and purlins should be fixed to the design, painted against rust and checked before any tiles go on.

  • Interiors in a working home

    Feature walls, ceilings, lighting and fitted kitchens are best planned together, so the electrical points, ceiling bulkheads and cabinets line up before the finishes start.

  • Approvals sit with the county

    Nakuru and Kericho counties each run their own building approvals. Your architect submits the drawings to the county where the plot sits, and the project is then registered with the NCA before work starts.
