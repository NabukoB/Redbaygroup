Building here

What to plan for in Nairobi

  • Small plots, building up

    Many plots around Nairobi are small, so maisonettes and storey houses are common. That means suspended slabs, stairs and stronger foundations, all of which need an engineer's design and careful supervision.

  • Check the soil first

    Parts of Nairobi's eastern side, Machakos and Kajiado have black cotton soil, which swells when wet and shrinks when dry. It needs a deeper or specially designed foundation. A soil check before design avoids cracked walls later.

  • Stone is close to hand

    Machine-cut stone from quarries around Nairobi is the usual walling here. It builds fast and straight, and the short haul keeps transport costs down.

  • Each county has its own approvals

    Nairobi City County, Kiambu and Machakos each run their own building approval process. Your architect submits the drawings to the county where the plot sits, and the project is then registered with the NCA before work starts.

FAQ

Common questions

Where in Nairobi are you based?

+

Our office is in Ruaraka, Nairobi. We work across Nairobi and the neighbouring counties, including Kiambu and Machakos.

Do you build from my architect's drawings?

+

Yes. We build to the approved drawings and work with your architect and engineer during construction. If you do not have drawings yet, we can advise on the next steps.

Can you build my house in phases?

+

Yes. Many clients build the foundation and shell first, then finishes later. We plan the phases so each stage is weather-safe and nothing has to be broken out later.

Are you licensed and insured?

+

Yes. Redbay Construction Ltd is registered with the National Construction Authority as a Category 7 contractor, and we hold Contractor's All Risk insurance on our projects.
