What to plan for in Diani and the coast

  • Salt air attacks metal

    Railings, balustrades and fixings near the sea need marine-grade stainless steel or properly galvanised and painted steel. Ordinary steel rusts and stains within a few years.

  • Coral stone and clean sand

    Coral stone keeps houses cool and gives the classic coastal finish, but it is porous and should be sealed. Sand for concrete and plaster must be washed: salty sand attacks reinforcement and causes damp walls.

  • Termites and timber

    Termites are common at the coast. Pergolas, trusses and doors should be treated hardwood, and the soil under the slab should be treated before it is cast.

  • Design for breeze and shade

    Cross ventilation, deep overhangs, balconies and rooftop pergolas keep a coastal home comfortable with less air conditioning.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you work at the coast?

+

Yes. We have built and finished homes in Diani and Ukunda in Kwale County. Tell us where your plot is and we will confirm.

I live in Nairobi or abroad. Can you build my coastal home?

+

Yes. We agree the scope, timeline and payment stages in writing before work starts, and each payment is tied to a stage of work being completed, so you can follow progress from wherever you are.

Which materials last best near the sea?

+

Sealed coral stone or well-rendered walls, marine-grade stainless steel or galvanised metalwork, treated hardwood and aluminium windows. Our coastal building guide explains each choice.

Do you also do interiors at the coast?

+

Yes. Redbay Interiors handles ceilings, feature walls, lighting and finishes, as in our Ukunda interiors project.
