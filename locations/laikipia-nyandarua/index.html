What to plan for in Nanyuki and Nyahururu

  • Keep the warmth in

    Nights are cold at this altitude. Ceilings with insulation, well-fitted doors and windows, and a fireplace or chimney where wanted make a big difference to comfort.

  • Roofs and gutters for heavy rain

    Steeper roofs, wide eaves, strong gutters and good drainage around the house keep water away from the walls and foundations in the long rains.

  • Stone for strength and warmth

    Stone walls are durable and hold heat, which suits the highland climate. Plastered or left exposed, they give a solid, low-maintenance finish.

  • Approvals in Laikipia and Nyandarua

    Plans are approved by the county where the plot sits. Allow time for the approval before planning your start date, then register the project with the NCA.
