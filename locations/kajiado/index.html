Building here

What to plan for in Kajiado

  • Black cotton soil

    Much of Kajiado has black cotton soil, which swells and shrinks with the seasons. Foundations need to go deeper, or be designed specially, so walls and slabs do not crack.

  • Plan for water

    Water can be scarce in dry months. Rainwater harvesting from roofs, enough storage tanks and good drainage around the buildings should be part of the design from the start.

  • Schools follow safety rules

    School buildings should meet the Ministry of Education's safety standards: classroom sizes, outward-opening doors, stairs at both ends of storey blocks and fire extinguishers in every block.

  • Building around the school calendar

    For a running school, noisy and risky work is planned for the holidays and the site is fenced off from learners, so lessons and exams carry on.
